What you'll learn

  • Pick the right market. Score languages on niche demand, competition, reachability, and reviewer availability — not raw size.
  • Own the process. Freeze your source, brief the AI properly, and hire one professional native reviewer per language.
  • Check what you can't read. Bilingual QA: mechanical passes you run tonight, plus an independent native sample check that catches tone failures.
  • Look native. Cover text, subtitles, punctuation conventions, page counts, and fonts that make the book read as local.
  • Protect yourself. Plain-English rights: who may translate, what to get in writing, and what "free" platform offers really cost.
  • Get read abroad. In-language keywords, review velocity, per-market channels, and price bands — the boring money details.

This handbook stays out of two neighbor subjects on purpose: everyday personal AI translation, and general wide-store distribution. See the README for how the three handbooks divide territory.
